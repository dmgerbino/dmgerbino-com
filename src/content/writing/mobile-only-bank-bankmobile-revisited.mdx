[BankMobile](https://www.bankmobile.com/) is another “Mobile First Bank Account.” They joined the ranks of [Simple](https://www.simple.com/), [Moven](https://www.moven.com/), [GoBank](https://m.gobank.com/welcome), etc. They officially launched on January 14, 2015. I was there for the launch [press conference](http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/customers-bank-launches-bankmobile-americas-first-and-only-no-fee-mobile-bank-300020125.html) and have gotten to know a few of the principles behind this mobile centric FDIC insured bank via parent [Customers Bank](https://www.snl.com/IRWebLinkX/corporateprofile.aspx?iid=4293992). Since their launch, they have been reaching out to their customers seeking feedback to make their banking experience better. This has culminated in a crowd sourcing [contest](http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bankmobile-launches-crowdsourcing-contest-300054784.html) launched on March 24, 2015. They are asking both their customers and noncustomers the following questions: “How would you build your own bank? Are you tired of the lack of innovation banks that offer today? What would it take for you to enjoy your banking experience?” The winner gets $5,000. (**Disclaimer**: I was honored to be asked to be one of the judges of the contest.) Additionally, on April 15, they announced Monica as the winner of their [$5k sweepstakes for email subscribers](https://www.bankmobile.com/pays-revolutionary-tips-recent-grads-looking-work/).

Since I first published my first impressions of the app, I have continued to experiment with it. I was surprised to get a few free checks to use when my debit card arrived. I’ve used my debit card as both a swipe and as a Google Wallet Point of Sale purchase. When I first started using BankMobile, I only opened a checking account instead of both a checking and savings account. I wanted to test opening a savings account at a later date. It was super easy. While they still have some UX and UI issues to resolve, the overall experience is better than most banks. My standard mobile banking app is the Chase Android app. It pales in comparison to BankMobile. The [Malauzai Software](http://malauzai.com/) team (the app developer) have outdone themselves. BankMobile and the FinTech startups Simple, Moven and GoBank continue to be leaders in the mobile banking space.

Here is the intro to my January 19th article published on The Financial Brand titled “BankMobile Introduces Fee-Free Branchless Banking”:

At a time when traditional banks are vying to capture the hearts and money of digital consumers, BankMobile joins the fray as the first mobile-only bank in the U.S. that has absolutely no fees. While not the first branchless bank, BankMobile hopes to profitably build scale with an easy-to-navigate platform and straightforward offer.

In a bold move by innovator and visionary Jay Sidhu, chief executive of Customers Bancorp, a Pennsylvania-based community bank that has 13 branches, BankMobile joins the ranks of Simple, Moven, GoBank, Amex Serve, etc. Unlike many of its competitors, however, BankMobile is an independent operating digital banking division of a real bank. With no fees and no branches, BankMobile hopes to financially empower Millennials, middle class Americans, the underbanked, Gen-X and tech-savvy Baby Boomers.

During the introductory press conference, Sidhu made it clear that he wanted BankMobile to be different from every other bank and credit union.
